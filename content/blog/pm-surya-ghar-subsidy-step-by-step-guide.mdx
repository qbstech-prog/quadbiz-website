The PM Surya Ghar subsidy has made rooftop solar more affordable than ever for Tamil Nadu homeowners — but the application process puts a lot of people off. This guide explains, in plain terms, what the scheme is, who qualifies, how much you can get, and the steps involved. All figures and portal steps below are indicative and can change, so treat them as a guide and confirm the current details before you rely on them.

## What the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is, in plain terms

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is a central government scheme that helps homeowners install rooftop solar by paying a direct subsidy towards the system. The aim is simple: lower the up-front cost so more homes generate their own clean power and cut their electricity bills. The subsidy is paid to you after your system is installed and inspected.

## Who's eligible

Under current guidelines, the scheme is aimed at residential consumers. You generally need to be:

- An **Indian homeowner** with your **own roof** to install on.
- The holder of a **valid electricity connection** (in Tamil Nadu, a TANGEDCO connection).
- A household that has **not previously availed** a rooftop solar subsidy.

Eligibility criteria are set by the government and can be updated, so we always confirm your specific eligibility at the start.

## How much you can get

The subsidy is linked to system size. Under current PM Surya Ghar guidelines, a typical 3 kW home system can attract up to ₹78,000, with the per-kilowatt support tapering for larger systems. These amounts are set by the government and are subject to change — we treat any number as indicative until it's confirmed for your application. You can see the current structure and estimate your benefit on our [subsidy page](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy).

## The application process, step by step

At a high level, the journey looks like this:

1. **Register on the national portal** with your details and electricity connection.
2. **System design and quote** — your installer designs a suitable system and gives you a fixed quote.
3. **Approval to install** through the portal / discom process.
4. **Installation** by a registered vendor.
5. **Net metering** — TANGEDCO installs a bi-directional meter and connects your system.
6. **Inspection and subsidy release** — after inspection, the subsidy is credited to your bank account.

The exact portal screens and order can change as the scheme evolves, so always follow the current instructions on the official portal — or let us handle it.

## Common mistakes and delays, and how to avoid them

- **Mismatched details** — the name and connection on the application must match your electricity account exactly.
- **Choosing an unregistered vendor** — the subsidy requires an eligible, registered installer.
- **Incomplete paperwork** — missing documents are the most common cause of delay.
- **Skipping net metering steps** — the subsidy release depends on proper net-metering completion.

Most delays come down to paperwork, not the technology.
