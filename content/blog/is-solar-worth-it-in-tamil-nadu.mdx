Solar salespeople rarely tell you when *not* to go solar. We will — because the honest answer is what actually helps you decide. So, is rooftop solar worth it in Tamil Nadu? For most homes with a decent roof and a real electricity bill, yes. But there are genuine exceptions, and it's worth knowing them before you spend anything.

## The short answer

Tamil Nadu gets plenty of sunshine, grid tariffs keep rising, and the PM Surya Ghar subsidy lowers the up-front cost. For a home with a reasonable monthly bill and an unshaded roof, solar usually pays for itself in a few years and then delivers largely free power for two decades. The caveats are about *your* roof and *your* usage — which is why we always survey before promising anything.

## How savings actually work

Your solar system generates power during the day. That generation offsets what you'd otherwise buy from TANGEDCO. With **net metering**, any surplus you export is credited against what you draw at night, so you're billed on the *net*. The bigger your daytime and overall consumption relative to your roof space, the more you save. (We explain net metering in detail in a separate guide.)

## What payback depends on

Payback — how long before savings cover the cost — is driven by a handful of factors:

- **Your bill size** — the higher your current bill, the faster solar pays back.
- **System size** — it must be matched to your consumption.
- **The subsidy** — the PM Surya Ghar [subsidy](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy) shortens payback meaningfully for eligible homes.
- **Shading and orientation** — a bright, unshaded roof generates more.

For many Tamil Nadu homes, typical payback lands in the region of three to five years, with 25-year panel life beyond that. These are typical ranges, not a guarantee — your numbers come from a survey.

## When solar is *less* worth it

Being straight with you:

- **Very small bills** — if your monthly bill is tiny, the savings may not justify the investment.
- **Heavily shaded roofs** — trees or taller neighbouring buildings that shade the roof for much of the day cut generation sharply.
- **Rented homes** — if you don't own the roof, the economics and eligibility get complicated.
- **No usable roof area** — some homes simply don't have the space for a worthwhile system.

In these cases we'll tell you honestly rather than sell you a system that won't perform.

## The long game: 25-year panel life vs rising tariffs

Solar is a hedge against rising electricity prices. Grid tariffs have trended upward for years; a solar system fixes a big part of your energy cost today for the next couple of decades. Quality panels typically carry 25-year performance warranties, so you're locking in savings well beyond the payback point.
